# Walmart Inc. (WMT)

## Business model
- Everyday-low-price retailer; price leadership is core to the model. [N524]
- Beyond merchandise it is building membership, advertising, marketplace, fulfillment and financial-services revenue. [N533]
- Sam's Club is membership-only ($50 Club, $110 Plus); fees are deferred and recognized ratably over one year. [N528][N592]

## Revenue build-up
- FY26 net sales: Walmart U.S. $483.0B (68%), International $130.4B (19%), Sam's Club $93.0B (13%). [N525][N526][N527]
- Walmart U.S. comps rose 3.3% in the July 2026 quarter. eCommerce contributed about 4.9 points, so non-online comps were weak. [N594][N595]
- Consolidated eCommerce grew 23% (+$8.2B), mainly through store- and club-fulfilled delivery. [N596]
- Membership fee revenue grew 17.4% in the April quarter, within a 27.0% rise in membership and other income. [N583]
- Sam's FY26 comps lost 1.9 points to lower fuel prices. [N573]
- International carries currency translation risk. [N542]

## Core thesis
- Revenue: growth depends on eCommerce delivery keeping comps up. Comps slowed from 4.3% to 3.3% quarter on quarter. [N581][N594]
- Margin up: management expects advertising and other higher-margin lines to grow operating income faster than sales. [N545] Sam's fee increases (effective May 1, 2026) lift income as they are recognized. [N584]
- Margin down: price investment continues through FY27 [N599]. Club-fulfilled delivery cut Sam's gross rate 26 bps [N586]. Liability claims rose $0.9B [N579]. Tariffs may have a material FY27 impact [N559]. The $2.9B tariff refund was one-time, with further refunds uncertain. [N598][N588]
- Guidance staleness: the notes' latest numeric sales/EPS guide is FY26 (Feb 2025 call), well over six months old [N1022][N1024]. The notes have no FY27 sales or EPS guide, only capex of $25–27B and a $0.99 dividend. [N577][N578]

## What changed over time
- 2021: the Asda and Seiyu sales moved International out of the UK and Japan. [N160][N161]
- 2022: operating income and adjusted EPS fell 20% in Q1 FY23, and $3.3B of opioid charges followed. [N723][N211]
- 2024: Vizio closed in December for about $2B net cash. [N410]
- 2025: FY26 guided to 3–4% sales growth and 3.5–5.5% operating income growth. [N1022][N1023] ROI hit 15.5%, the highest since 2016. [N1017]
- 2026: eCommerce became the main comp driver (4.3 points). [N574] Sam's raised fees, and $2.9B of tariff refunds arrived in the July quarter. [N600][N598]

## Catalysts
- Fiscal year-end 2027-01-31: Q4 is seasonally the highest-sales quarter, and the FY27 capex guide covers the year. [N530][N577]
- CBP tariff-refund outcomes: undated. [N588]
- Sam's fee increase recognized ratably over the following year. [N584][N592]
- Flipkart and Mexico (COFECE) competition outcomes: undated. [N460][N493]

## Potentially useful datasets
- Store- and club-fulfilled delivery volumes: they track the eCommerce comp driver [N591] and are observable before quarterly reports.
- Store foot traffic: it tracks in-store transactions, and weekly data arrives ahead of comps. [N537][N554]
- Retail fuel prices and volumes: they proxy Sam's fuel comp effect, and pump prices are public in real time. [N603]
- U.S. general-merchandise import volumes: they track the tariff-exposed cost base ahead of margin effects. [N569]
- Web/shelf rollback prices: they track the price-investment driver. [N1000]
