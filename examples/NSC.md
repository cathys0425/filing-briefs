# NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORP (NSC)

## Business model
- Hauls raw materials, intermediate products and finished goods by rail, mainly in the Southeast, East and Midwest, and interchanges with other carriers for the rest of the U.S. [N451]
- About 95% of revenue sits under negotiated fuel surcharges, so fuel prices pass through to revenue. [N525]
- About 90% of revenue is exempt or contracted, with the rest under public tariffs. [N457]
- Positions its intermodal network as the largest in the eastern U.S. CSX is the primary rival. [N496][N464]

## Revenue build-up
- Revenue is units times average revenue per unit (RPU) across merchandise, intermodal and coal. 2025 revenue was about $12.2B. [N481][N452]
- Merchandise is about 63% of revenue on 2.3M carloads. Q3 2025 growth came from volume plus pricing and mix. [N453][N438]
- In 2026, chemicals rose on NGL, sand and petroleum. Metals and construction fell as customer facilities were idled. Autos fell on supply constraints. [N513][N514][N531]
- Intermodal is about 25% of revenue on 4.1M units. In 2026, domestic grew on tight truck capacity while international fell as East Coast port volumes dropped. [N454][N528][N529]
- Coal is about 12% of revenue, with utility demand anchored by 18 directly served plants. Utility tonnage rose 27% in Q1 2026. Export tonnage rose on metallurgical coal output and seaborne pricing. [N455][N456][N499][N530]

## Core thesis
- Revenue: 2026 growth comes from higher RPU, driven by fuel surcharges, plus volume. Fuel surcharge revenue is expected to keep rising at current fuel prices. [N526][N534]
- Margins: fuel lifts expenses as well as revenue. Adjusted operating ratio (OR) improved from 68.7% in Q1 to 65.5% in Q2 2026 but remained above 63.4% in Q2 2025. Falling headcount supports cost control. [N527][N497][N516][N429][N517][N533]
- Downside: STB action could curb pricing freedom, and the merger could be delayed, denied or conditioned. Eastern Ohio costs may exceed accruals. [N469][N537][N468]

## What changed over time
- 2023: East Palestine costs hit results, and the railway OR was 76.5% for the year. [N251][N371]
- 2024: OR improved to 66.4% GAAP (65.8% adjusted). The Cincinnati Southern line purchase closed in March. [N397][N373]
- 2025: The July 28 Union Pacific merger agreement became the dominant corporate event. Q1 2025 included an insurance net benefit of $185M. [N446][N933]
- 2026: The initial STB application was deemed incomplete and a revised one is being prepared. Buybacks stay suspended. Eastern Ohio cash payments hit $300M in Q1. [N467][N538][N505]

## Catalysts
- STB review of the revised merger application, undated. Terms are one UP share plus $88.82 cash per NS share. [N467][N537][N461]
- Rest of 2026: fuel surcharge revenue and fuel expense both expected to rise year over year. [N534][N535]
- November 1, 2029: labor moratorium ends. Until then, new mandatory bargaining notices are barred. [N491][N523]

## Potentially useful datasets
- East and West Coast port container throughput. It tracks the international intermodal shift, and port discharge precedes the rail move. [N539][N529]
- Seaborne thermal coal prices and U.S. coal export shipments. They track export tonnage and coal RPU, and price moves precede shipments. [N524][N872]
- Power-generation and natural gas price data. They track utility coal tonnage, and gas prices and load precede coal burn and restocking. [N450][N499]
- Weekly U.S. rail carload data. It tracks carload volume and is published before quarterly results. [N479]
- Truckload rate index and tender rejections. They track domestic intermodal pricing, and truck conditions set the rate environment. [N937][N919]
