# MICRON TECHNOLOGY INC (MU)

## Business model
- Sells memory and storage as components, modules, SSDs, managed NAND, multi-chip packages and wafers. [N573]
- Strategic agreements are multi-year take-or-pay with binding volumes, and most carry min/max price bands. Other contracts are short-term. [N574][N575]
- Customers posted $12.75B of deposits, returnable if purchase minimums are missed. [N623]
- Largest deals have a price ceiling near Q2 2026 market levels and a floor. [N559]
- Most sales are in USD while much of the cost base is foreign-currency. [N619]

## Revenue build-up
- FY2026 DRAM was $100.68B (+252%: ASP ~+180%, bits mid-20%). NAND was $31.79B (+274%: ASP ~+200%, bits mid-20%). [N577][N578][N624][N625]
- Units: CMBU $43.09B, CDBU $37.59B (+420%), MCBU $36.60B, AEBU $15.89B. MCBU gains were price-led, with bits redirected to other units. [N579][N580][N581][N582][N626][N627]
- Data center is ~60% of revenue. The top ten customers are over half, and ~70% ships outside the US. [N592][N593][N596]
- Gross margin was 81% (85% in Q3) versus 40% in FY2025. [N628][N564]

## Core thesis
- Revenue rises only while supply stays short of demand and AI demand holds. Price bands and take-or-pay volumes cushion this. [N570][N608][N559][N621]
- Revenue and margins fall if weak demand or competitor supply cuts ASPs. ASPs have ranged from about +200% to −50% over five years. [N588][N591]
- Margins also depend on cost per gigabit, and fixed costs magnify lower utilization. Net capex of ~$25B in H1 2027 precedes the capacity due mid-2027. [N489][N491][N629][N630]
- Latest guidance is the 10-K of 2026-09-03, under six months old. The notes give capex guidance only. [N629]

## What changed over time
- 2023: China's CAC barred critical-infrastructure buyers from Micron products. FY2023 DRAM sales fell 51% and NAND 46%. [N267][N300][N301]
- 2024: Data center passed half of revenue and HBM sold out for 2024–25. Gross margin recovered from −1% in Q1 to 35% in Q4. [N412][N871][N338][N429]
- 2025: CMBU became the largest unit at $13.52B. 2026 capex guidance rose to ~$20B. [N897][N477][N534]
- 2026: Pricing shifted to take-or-pay agreements with deposits, and net capex guidance rose to ~$27B. [N621][N623][N565]

## Catalysts

- H1 2027: net capex of ~$25B. [N629]

- Mid-2027: Boise first DRAM wafers and Tongluo, Taiwan shipments. [N630][N632]

- Undated: Section 232 and 301 trade actions could bring tariffs. [N612][N613]

## Potentially useful datasets
- DRAM and NAND contract and spot price indices track ASP, the main revenue driver. Prices are observable before quarterly reports. [N635][N556]
- Server shipments and cloud deployment volumes track CMBU and CDBU demand and show volume ahead of filings. [N590]
- Data center build-out spending and power capacity additions track data center demand. Capex is committed before memory orders. [N606][N893]
- Smartphone unit shipments track MCBU volume. [N910]
- China and Hong Kong memory import data track the CAC-ban revenue hit. [N299]
