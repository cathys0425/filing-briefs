# APPLIED MATERIALS INC /DE (AMAT)

## Business model
- Sells equipment, services and software to semiconductor and related industries, mainly through a direct sales force [N572][N573].
- Semiconductor Systems supplies the largest share of revenue and profit [N498]. AGS earns recurring revenue from services, spares and factory automation software, and is shifting toward subscriptions [N485][N574].
- Equipment is built to customer specifications, so cancellations cost money that cannot be recovered [N499].

## Revenue build-up
- Semiconductor Systems: Q3 FY26 revenue was $7,040M (77% of the total, +27%), driven by DRAM transitions and leading-edge foundry/logic [N575][N576]. Sales split across foundry/logic, DRAM and NAND [N488]. DRAM was 29% of the segment in Q2 FY26 [N562].
- AGS: Q3 FY26 revenue was $1,781M (+22%), from long-term service agreements and spares [N577]. Demand follows the installed base [N477].
- Geography: China was 28% of Q3 revenue ($2,506M), down from 35% [N579]. About 89% of FY25 revenue came from outside the U.S., and two customers were about 19% and 15% [N500][N492].

## Core thesis
- Revenue rises with chipmaker capex, which management ties to HBM, advanced packaging, AI and non-leading-edge nodes. Services grow with the installed base and renewals [N534][N535]. AI demand is hard to forecast [N501].
- Gross margin rose to 50.3% on higher selling prices and lower costs [N580]. It falls if U.S. export licensing limits China sales, as it has in the past, or if tariffs bite [N584][N569]. The tax rate rises on the global minimum tax [N581].
- Guidance is stale. The newest quantitative guidance in these notes is the 2025-05-15 call (Q3 FY25), over 18 months old [N876][N878]. The latest document is the 10-Q for the period ended 2026-07-26 [N575].

## What changed over time
- 2022: October U.S. export rules put a net $1.5–2B of FY23 China revenue at risk [N715][N716][N717].
- 2023: Memory was a down year, and China rose to 44% of Q4 revenue on trailing-edge DRAM shipments [N736][N781][N782].
- 2024: Gate-all-around revenue exceeded $2.5B and advanced packaging reached close to $1.7B [N846][N847].
- 2025: China revenue fell 18% in nine months, and DRAM's share of Semiconductor Systems fell to 22% in Q3 [N474][N472]. FY25 gross margin was 48.7% [N532].
- 2026: Semiconductor Systems fell 8% in Q1, then rose 27% in Q3 [N545][N575]. A $253M export-compliance charge was settled [N583].

## Catalysts
- The 25% CHIPS credit applies to investments that begin construction before 2027. It was later raised to 35%, but the notes do not state whether the deadline changed [N431][N538].
- Singapore conditional reduced tax rates begin expiring in fiscal 2030 [N512].
- The February 2026 transition tax payment, the September 2026 credit agreement expiry and the spring 2026 EPIC opening have already passed [N463][N570][N882].

## KPIs to track
- Semiconductor Systems revenue by foundry/logic, DRAM and NAND [N488].
- Gross margin (50.3% in Q3 FY26) and AGS operating margin (29.2% in Q2 FY26) [N580][N565].
- China share of revenue [N579].
- Backlog; about 31% was not expected to fill within 12 months at FY25 year-end [N491].
- Days sales outstanding (77) [N578].
- Subscription share of service revenue [N872].

## Potentially useful datasets
- Wafer starts and fab utilization: track AGS spares and services demand. Usage drives spares consumption ahead of reported revenue [N585].
- Hyperscaler capex: tracks AI-linked leading-edge foundry and memory equipment demand. Customer spending plans precede tool orders [N885].
- HBM share of DRAM wafer starts: tracks HBM and advanced packaging demand, since allocation decisions precede equipment orders [N823].
- Customer fab construction announcements: track equipment demand timing, because construction precedes tool installation [N513].
