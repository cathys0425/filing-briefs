# NVIDIA CORP (NVDA)

## Business model
- Data center-scale AI infrastructure company with two segments, Compute & Networking and Graphics [N606].
- Data Center is sold as rack-scale systems, subsystems or modules bundled with software. Paid software licenses and IP licensing add to this [N533][N534][N535].
- Most sales are cancellable purchase orders. Pricing does not track short-term costs [N549][N575].
- Under the new AI cloud partner model, NVIDIA sells infrastructure to AI clouds and commits to cloud service agreements that the clouds can resell [N613].

## Revenue build-up
- FY26 revenue was $215.9B (+65%). Data Center rose 68%: compute +59% on Blackwell, networking +142% on NVLink for GB200/GB300 [N576][N577][N578][N579].
- Q2 FY27 Data Center was $89.0B (+117%) on Blackwell Ultra. Hyperscalers are about half of Data Center; the rest is AI clouds, industrial, enterprise and sovereign [N607][N594]. ACIE grew 138% [N610].
- Edge Computing: Blackwell workstations were partly offset by weak consumer PCs on high memory prices. Gaming faces supply constraints [N611][N584].
- Concentration: three direct customers were 21%, 17% and 16% of Q1 FY27 revenue [N595]. Non-US-headquartered customers fell to 22% from 42%, and Hopper China shipments went to zero from $4.6B [N596][N597].

## Core thesis
- Revenue rises only if customers can secure data centers, energy and capital. Shortfalls delay shipments and shift revenue timing [N603][N620]. Rubin is the next ramp, and new-architecture bring-up has caused delays before [N604].
- Margin: FY26 gross margin fell to 71.1% from 75.0% on Blackwell system mix, with provisions of $7.2B versus $3.7B [N582][N583]. Q2 FY27 recovered to 75.0% on Blackwell Ultra mix [N612]. Direction is up if mix holds and down if ramps or provisions slip.
- Staleness: the last numeric guidance in the notes is Q1 FY26, from February 2025 [N901]. The latest forward statement, Rubin in H2 FY27, dates from the April 2026 10-Q, about seven months old [N599].

## What changed over time
- 2022: Q2 FY23 revenue of $6.7B missed the $8.1B outlook, with a $1.22B inventory charge as crypto and gaming demand faded [N164][N186].
- 2023: Data Center became the engine, with Q3 FY24 revenue +279%. New export controls took effect October 23, 2023 [N296][N318].
- 2024: Blackwell production shipments were set to start in Q4 FY25. FY24 Data Center revenue was $47.5B (+217%) [N407][N352].
- 2025: The H20 license requirement forced a $4.5B charge and 60.5% gross margin in Q1 FY26. Q3 FY26 revenue reached $57.0B [N491][N495][N515].
- 2026: NVIDIA says it is effectively foreclosed from China data center compute. Rubin production shipments began in Q3 FY27 [N544][N617].

## Catalysts
- Q3 FY27 results, the first quarter with Rubin production shipments. The date is not in the notes [N617].
- Fiscal 2027 capex is expected to rise, and Gaming supply constraints continue [N585][N584].
- China: no H200 revenue yet, and shipments face a 25% US tariff. The AI Diffusion replacement rule and the 15%+ government revenue share are unpublished and undated [N602][N545][N564].
- Ohio campus guarantee exposure (capped at $105B) starts rising in fiscal 2029 [N618][N619].

## KPIs to track
- Data Center revenue, compute versus networking, and hyperscale share [N607][N594].
- Gross margin and inventory/purchase-obligation provisions [N612][N583].
- Direct-customer concentration and non-US share [N595][N596].
- Supply and capacity commitments ($279B), receivables and operating cash flow [N616][N621].

## Potentially useful datasets
- Data center construction, power and capital availability: tracks the deployment constraint on shipment timing, and sites and power must exist before systems ship [N622][N561].
- Hyperscaler capex disclosures: track hyperscaler demand, about half of Data Center, and are set before NVIDIA recognizes revenue [N843][N594].
- Cloud GPU instance availability and pricing: tracks cloud-provider demand and shows tightness before reported numbers [N436][N895].
- Taiwan and South Korea chip export data: tracks supply availability, upstream of NVIDIA shipments [N573].
- Memory and system price indices: track consumer PC demand pressure in Edge Computing [N605].
