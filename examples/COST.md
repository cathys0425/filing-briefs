# COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP /NEW (COST)

## Business model
- Low prices on a limited assortment (under 4,000 active SKUs per core warehouse) drive volume and fast inventory turns, allowing lower gross margins than most retailers. [N477][N487]
- Membership fees ($65 U.S. annual) are recognized ratably over one year. Executive members, up to 42,300 from 38,700 (units as reported), were about 75.2% of net sales. [N478][N509][N480][N481]
- U.S. and Canada produced 86% of net sales and 83% of operating income. [N492][N493]

## Revenue build-up
- Net sales = core merchandise + ancillary businesses (gasoline, pharmacy) + other businesses (e-commerce, travel). FY2026 net sales were $297.2B, up 10.1%. [N510][N745]
- Comps = ticket × frequency; in FY2026 ticket contributed more. Q3 comps were 9.8% reported, 6.6% adjusted for gasoline and FX. [N511][N736]
- Gasoline is 11% of sales; a 12% rise in price per gallon added $3,501M (130 bp). Digitally enabled sales are 11% of sales. [N484][N512][N485]

## Core thesis
- Revenue: headline growth runs about three points above adjusted growth because of gasoline and FX. If pump prices or currency reverse, reported sales and comps fall even if traffic holds; lower gasoline prices also reverse gross-margin dilution. [N736][N512][N442]
- Margins: the membership fee tailwind is fading, with the fee increase at about 30% of membership income growth versus about 40% in 2025. Renewal is diluted by international and online sign-ups. [N515][N521]
- Margins: operating margins are very thin, tariffs are more likely to hurt than help, and top-of-scale wages rose $1.00/hour in March 2026. Kirkland Signature mix is the offset. [N520][N519][N488][N496]

## What changed over time
- 2024: U.S./Canada fee raised to $65 on September 1, with about $370M expected over two years. Comps were frequency-led. [N313][N339][N947]
- 2025: renewal slid to 92.3% (U.S./Canada) and 89.8% worldwide. Entry wage rose to at least $20/hour. [N394][N399]
- 2026: gasoline turned from first-half headwind to full-year tailwind. Ticket overtook frequency as comp driver. A one-time $0.15 EPS tariff-refund benefit lifted Q4. [N512][N511][N749]

## Catalysts
- January 2027: $4,000M buyback authorization expires. [N472]
- December 2026: Q1 FY2027 results, a 12-week quarter. [N711][N712]
- FY2027: capex of about $7.5B and up to 33 openings, including five relocations. [N516][N517]

## KPIs to track
- Comps adjusted for gasoline and FX, split into ticket and frequency. [N748][N511]
- Renewal rates (U.S./Canada and worldwide), paid members, and Executive count. [N513][N468][N480]

## Potentially useful datasets
- U.S. retail gasoline price per gallon: tracks the gasoline sales effect and gross-margin dilution. [N476][N442]
- E-commerce web traffic and app engagement: tracks digitally enabled comps. [N742][N752]
