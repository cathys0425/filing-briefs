# LOWES COMPANIES INC (LOW)

## Business model
- Omnichannel home-improvement retailer with 1,759 U.S. stores and about 196M sq ft of selling space at FY2025 year-end. [N447][N452]
- The Total Home strategy grows Pro, Online and Home Services customers. Small-to-medium Pros are served mainly through stores. Larger residential and commercial Pros are served through branches. [N505][N450][N451]
- Lowe's is importer of record and pays tariffs directly. Installation runs through independent contractors. [N512][N448]

## Revenue build-up
- Comps are transactions × average ticket. In Q2 FY26, comps rose 0.2%: ticket rose 2.3% to $107.90 and transactions fell 2.1%. [N506][N509]
- DIY was about 70% of revenue as of Feb 2025, so big-ticket discretionary demand dominates. Nine of 13 categories grew in Q2 on Pro and online repair demand. [N740][N510]
- Online added about 195 bp to Q2 comps, against about 85 bp a year earlier. [N507]
- The FBM and ADG acquisitions drove most of FY2025's 3.1% sales growth. Q1 FY26 sales rose 10.3% to $23.1B. [N479][N492]
- Sales peak in Q2 and trough in Q4. [N455]

## Core thesis
- Revenue: ticket has carried comps while transactions fell (-2.8% in FY2025, -2.1% in Q2 FY26). Volume growth needs a housing-turnover recovery. Management expects lower rates to help, but it still cites persistent DIY discretionary weakness. [N478][N506][N442][N515]
- Margins: Q2 gross margin was pressured by the acquisitions' cost structure and fuel. About $80M of tariff refunds helped, with future refunds uncertain. ROIC fell to 25.5% from 29.5%. The 2024 rule of thumb was 14–15 bp of operating margin per comp point below guidance. Margin direction is down unless comps or integration improve. [N513][N514][N511][N678][N719]
- The last sales and comp guidance in these notes (FY2025: $83.5–84.5B, comps flat to +1%) dates from Feb 2025, more than six months old. The newest filing, the 10-Q of 2026-07-31, guides only capex at about $2.5B. [N747][N516]

## What changed over time
- 2023: The Canada sale closed Feb 3, leaving a U.S.-only business. [N201][N253]
- 2023: Comps ran -4.3%, -1.6% and -7.4% through Q3, and full-year sales guidance fell from $88–90B to about $86B. [N232][N246][N261][N628][N664]
- 2024: Guidance was cut twice. Fiscal comps fell 2.7% on a 3.0% transaction decline, and the mortgage lock-in pushed housing turnover near mid-1990s lows. [N713][N731][N388][N718]
- 2025: Lowe's closed ADG ($1.3B) and FBM ($8.8B). Comps turned positive in Q2–Q4 after -1.7% in Q1. [N427][N441][N484]
- 2026: Tariff exposure became direct, with IEEPA refunds beginning in Q2. ROIC fell to 25.5% from 29.5%. [N512][N514][N511]

## Catalysts
- Q3 FY26 results: the Q3 FY25 10-Q was filed 2025-10-31, but no Q3 FY26 document is in these notes. [N434]
- FY2026 year-end results and FY2027 guidance: the prior year-end was Jan 30, 2026, so the next is roughly late Jan 2027 (inferred). [N452]
- IEEPA refund collections: amount and timing are uncertain and undated. [N514]
- FBM and ADG integration: expected to capture more planned Pro spend over the long term. [N517]
- Lower interest rates: management expects them to lift demand, with no date given. [N442]

## Potentially useful datasets
- Store foot traffic or transaction counts: track comparable transactions, the weak driver, and are observable before the quarterly filing. [N518][N432]
- Web and app traffic: track online sales, which management says grow on higher traffic and conversion. [N462][N445]
- Existing-home sales and housing turnover: track big-ticket DIY demand, which rates suppress. [N401][N461]
- Appliance online prices and availability: track the appliance category and ticket pressure. [N504][N670]
- Regional weather: tracks seasonal category timing. [N433]
