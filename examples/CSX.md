# CSX CORP (CSX)

## Business model
- Class I eastern railroad (~20,000 route miles, 26 states) that sets its own rates under the Staggers Act. [N369][N488]
- Four lines: merchandise, intermodal, coal and trucking (Quality Carriers). Other revenue is demurrage, storage and switching. [N460][N466][N471]
- Fuel surcharges pass fuel cost into revenue. Coal contracts follow benchmark prices and carry floors. [N530][N911][N946]

## Revenue build-up
- 2025: merchandise $8.8B (62%, 2.6M carloads); intermodal $2.1B (15%, 3.0M units); coal $1.9B (13%, 718k carloads); trucking $816M (6%); other ~4%. [N461][N462][N463][N464][N471]
- Merchandise: price plus volume. Pricing offset volume declines in 2025. [N418][N490]
- Intermodal: international follows port volumes; domestic follows customer wins and the truck market. [N492][N546]
- Coal: export follows seaborne prices and supply; domestic follows natural gas prices. [N476][N478]
- Trucking: rates plus fuel surcharges. [N444]
- 2025 revenue fell 3% on export coal prices, volume and fuel recovery. [N490]

## Core thesis
- Revenue rises only if merchandise pricing and intermodal volume outrun export coal benchmark and fuel-recovery drag, which caused the 2025 decline. Domestic intermodal and export coal volume were growing in Q2–Q3 2026. [N490][N546][N549]
- Margins fall while fuel cost outpaces surcharges: Q3 2026 locomotive fuel prices rose 67% and fuel cost rose $170M. [N554][N530]
- Margins rise only if service recovers: Q3 2026 velocity fell 2%, dwell rose 12% and trip plans worsened. Management sizes lost revenue at ~$1M+ per day. Capex below $2.4B and a ~$40M annual depreciation benefit help. [N550][N551][N947][N553][N497]

## What changed over time
- 2021: Quality Carriers closed July 1, adding ~6% to revenue. [N61][N67]
- 2022: growth was price-led; Q2 coal revenue rose 54% on 3% lower volume. [N692]
- 2024: Key Bridge collapse cost $25–30M of net revenue per month; Helene cost ~$50M in Q4. [N847][N893]
- 2025: Q1 coal revenue fell 27%; Quality Carriers took a $164M Q3 impairment after $108M in Q4 2024. [N417][N404][N456]
- 2026: fuel became the main cost headwind and service reliability worsened through the year. [N537][N554][N550]

## Catalysts
- Norfolk Southern/Union Pacific merger pending STB approval (no date given); it could reshape CSX's competitive landscape. [N469]
- Remaining unions still negotiating under prior contracts. [N556]
- 2026 capex below $2.4B; Blue Ridge, reopened September 2025 after ~$470M of 2025 capital spending, lowers property additions. [N553][N499][N523]
- Howard Street Tunnel completion by end-2025 enables double-stack service. [N923]

## Potentially useful datasets
- US port container volumes: tracks international intermodal volume. Port throughput should lead rail volume. [N458][N500]
- Truckload spot rates and capacity: tracks domestic intermodal conversion. A tighter truck market preceded wins. [N559][N546]
- Seaborne coal price indices: track export coal revenue per unit. Contracts follow benchmarks with a lag. [N542][N911][N875]
- Diesel price series: tracks fuel cost and surcharge revenue. Price moves precede reported expense. [N558]
- North American light-vehicle production: tracks automotive carloads. [N457]
- Natural gas prices: track domestic coal volume through coal-fired generation. [N514]
