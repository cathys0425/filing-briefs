# COCA COLA CO (KO)

## Business model
- Local bottling partners produce and distribute the brands; economics are mostly local. [N274][N267]
- Under incidence pricing, company revenue is a percentage of bottlers' revenue, which aligns incentives. [N46][N62]
- Company-owned bottlers are being refranchised; Bottling Investments Group is under 20% of net revenue. [N196][N192]

## Revenue build-up
- Organic growth is unit case volume plus price/mix. Q1 2025 volume was +2% and organic revenue +6%. [N278][N279]
- Price/mix drove most of Q1 growth, with pricing partly offset by unfavorable mix. [N275]
- Highly inflationary markets added much less to Q1 price/mix than in 2024; management expects this pricing to moderate. [N276][N248]
- Concentrate sales lagged unit cases in Q1 because of two fewer days, partly offset by shipment timing. [N277]
- fairlife is vertically integrated, so its growth adds revenue mechanically, and its sales have passed $1 billion. [N240][N245]

## Core thesis
- Revenue: the 2025 guide is 5-6% organic growth, targeted at roughly 2-3 points each from volume and price. With inflationary-market pricing fading, volume must hold near 2%. [N281][N258][N276][N278]
- EPS: currency-neutral growth of 7-9% becomes 2-3% all-in because of a 5-6 point currency headwind. A currency move changes reported EPS directly. [N281][N282][N283]
- Margins: agricultural commodity cost pressure and a higher tax rate (20.8%) weigh on EPS, while productivity gains weighted to H2 offset them. [N268][N261][N288]

## Catalysts
- End-2025: fairlife capacity expansion, after which growth may be less constrained. [N287]
- H2 2025: productivity benefits expected. [N288]
- H1 2025: roughly $6.1 billion fairlife contingent payment, raising net interest expense. [N249]
- 2025: final $1.2 billion transition tax payment. [N271]
- A $6 billion IRS deposit is under appeal. [N251]

## KPIs to track
- Unit case volume growth against concentrate sales growth, including shipment-day effects. [N278][N277]
- Price/mix and its inflationary-market component. [N275][N276]
- About 14 million coolers across about 33 million outlets. [N265]
- Low- or no-calorie share of volume, about 30%. [N280]
- 2025 free cash flow of about $9.5 billion (guidance). [N260]

## Potentially useful datasets
- Container shipping bookings, late May to early June: a proxy for U.S. supply chain disruption affecting consumer categories. Bookings precede the disruption reaching consumers. [N289][N286]
- Cooler placements and visible cold inventory: track the availability driver behind volume. Placements precede unit case growth. [N272][N256]
- Aluminum spot or contract prices: track North American can cost pressure that packaging mix could offset. Input prices move before reported costs. [N273]
- Scanner-based retail sales for Trademark Coca-Cola and fairlife: track retail sales growth and share gains. Scanner data is available before the company reports. [N255]
- Argentina, Turkey and African consumer price inflation: tracks the hyperinflationary pricing contribution to price/mix. The company prices to offset local inflation, so inflation should lead pricing. [N191][N172]
