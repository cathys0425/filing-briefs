# COCA COLA CO (KO)

## Business model
- Sells concentrate to local bottlers under incidence pricing, so revenue is a percentage of bottlers' marketplace revenue [N64][N57]. Some businesses (certain juice, Costa, BIG) are sold as finished goods [N65], which carry lower margins [N8].
- Refranchising raised $3.5bn of gross proceeds in 2024 [N268]. Bottling Investments was already under 20% of net revenue in 2023 [N197]. fairlife consolidation is a vertically integrated business that mechanically lifts revenue [N254].
- Roughly half at-home, half away-from-home [N104].

## Revenue build-up
- Organic revenue = unit case volume + price/mix. Concentrate sales can diverge from unit cases on shipment timing (4 points in Q2 2024) [N243].
- Q4 2024: organic +14%, price/mix +9% (about 8 points pricing, including inflationary pricing in devaluation markets), volume +2% [N270][N269][N271]. Q3 2024 volume was -1% [N256].
- About half of North America's 11-point Q2 2024 price/mix was mix, not pricing [N244].
- Refranchising cuts comparable revenue growth 4-5 points and EPS about 2 points [N234].
- fairlife volume grew 15% in 2023 [N207]. Further growth needs a new New York factory [N282].

## Core thesis
- **Staleness:** the latest guidance is the 2025-02-11 call, about 21 months old, and no later notes exist. Everything below is a 2025 view.
- **Revenue:** 2025 organic growth of about 5-6% is weighted toward price [N275], with volume somewhat better than 2024 [N280]. This needs volume to improve, because inflationary pricing is hard to forecast [N195].
- **Margins/EPS:** EPS lags revenue. Guidance is 2-3% EPS growth [N274], against a 6-7 point currency headwind [N276], a tax rate rising to 20.8% [N277] and only modest gross margin expansion [N279]. Aluminum up about 25% adds cost pressure [N283]. Currency relief would lift EPS; lower-income consumer pressure would cut volume [N284].

## What changed over time
- 2021: the IRS dispute became a risk, with about $12bn of potential liability [N10][N11].
- 2022: price/mix took over from reopening volume. Q3 organic was +16% on 4% volume, with gross margin down about 190bp [N124][N126].
- 2023: volume stalled (flat in Q2) while price/mix ran 9-11% [N176][N177]. Hyperinflation markets added about 2.5 points in Q3 [N187].
- 2024: a $760m BODYARMOR impairment [N231], and the fairlife payment estimate rose to $6.1bn from $5.3bn [N263][N251].
- 2025: EPS growth guided down to 2-3% [N274].

## Catalysts
- None of the notes dates an event after 2026-11-21. The February 2025 guidance, the H1 2025 fairlife payment [N263] and the year-end 2024 IRS appeal [N250] are all past.
- Undated and unconfirmed: the IRS case outcome [N250] and the New York fairlife factory start [N282].

## KPIs to track
- Unit case volume versus price/mix, and concentrate-versus-unit-case gap [N243].
- Share of price/mix from inflationary pricing [N242].
- Currency headwind to revenue and EPS [N276].
- Comparable gross margin [N126].
- FCF conversion (93% in 2024; target 90-95%) [N272][N58].
- fairlife volume [N207]; outlet coverage (about 33m) [N273].

## Potentially useful datasets
- Retail scanner data for Trademark Coca-Cola and fairlife: tracks North American at-home sales [N266]. It reflects sell-out, so it can lead reported volume when concentrate shipments run on timing [N243].
- Cooler placements: track a cited consumption driver [N286] and distribution expansion [N137]. Placements precede the volume they enable.
- Western Europe foodservice foot traffic: tracks away-from-home pressure [N247]. It is observable before quarterly volume.
- Sweetener and juice commodity indices: track input costs [N166]. They lead COGS as hedges roll off [N133].
