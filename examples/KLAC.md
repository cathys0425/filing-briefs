# KLA CORP (KLAC)

## Business model
- Sells process control and yield management products to semiconductor makers, mostly through a direct sales force; wafer inspection is about 49% of FY26 revenue [N649][N620][N688].
- Services (about 23% of FY26 revenue) are recurring, subscription-like maintenance contracts on installed tools [N619][N681].
- The customer base is concentrated (TSMC above 10% of revenue), and orders are generally cancellable or delayable [N685][N651][N653].

## Revenue build-up
- Product revenue follows customer capex and new orders, with manufacturing-cycle lags [N593]. Semiconductor Process Control was about 90% of Q1 FY26 revenue and grew 12% in FY26 on AI/HPC foundry/logic and memory [N580][N684].
- Services = installed base × utilization × renewal rate. They grew 16% in FY26, but only 4% sequentially in the September 2026 quarter [N686][N687][N701].
- Regions: Korea +26.2% in FY26 on HBM/DRAM; China 30% of FY26 revenue but 34.5% of the September 2026 quarter on mature-node foundry [N690][N634][N703].
- Backlog was $12.57B at June 2026, against $7.86B a year earlier [N624].

## Core thesis
- Revenue rises if AI/HPC spending on leading-edge logic, memory and advanced packaging continues; management expects revenue growth in fiscal 2027 [N625][N691][N698]. Latest guidance (10-Q to 2026-09-30) is under six months old and is qualitative only [N704].
- Backlog is cancellable and can be pushed out, so a pushout would cut revenue [N629][N618]. China export-license failures could reduce backlog and force deposit returns [N639].
- Margins fall if DRAM costs, tariffs or lower-priced products persist: DRAM costs weigh into fiscal 2027, and gross margin was 61.9% in the September 2026 quarter [N661][N643][N652][N702]. Pillar Two adds $94.5M of fiscal 2027 tax expense [N705].

## What changed over time
- **2022:** October export rules and a planned ~20% fall in 2023 WFE ended the upcycle [N240][N840].
- **2024:** The March decision to exit Display; China peaked at 43% of FY24 revenue [N480][N634].
- **2025:** Backlog fell to $7.86B and China to 33%, while Taiwan rose to 27% of revenue on AI-linked leading-edge demand [N517][N634][N566].
- **2026:** Backlog rose to $12.57B on AI demand; a PCB forecast cut triggered a goodwill impairment [N624][N695].

## Catalysts
- BIS Affiliates Rule suspension runs to November 2026; what follows is not in the notes [N640].
- Section 232 semiconductor-import investigation is pending, with no date given [N641].
- Revolver ($1.50B) matures June 8, 2027 [N336].
- Fiscal 2027 Pillar Two expense of $94.5M, reversible if the Side-by-Side Safe Harbor is adopted [N705].

## Potentially useful datasets
- DRAM spot and contract prices: track the DRAM cost pressure on gross margin; input prices move before reported margin [N666][N660].
- China-bound equipment customs data and Entity List additions: track China share and export exposure; shipments and listings precede revenue and license outcomes [N648][N537].
- Korean memory capex and HBM capacity announcements: track Korea revenue; capex precedes tool orders [N696].
- AI data-center and leading-edge foundry capex: track demand for inspection tools; spending precedes orders given manufacturing lags [N665][N589][N593].
- Fab tool-installation counts: track the installed base behind service revenue [N575].
