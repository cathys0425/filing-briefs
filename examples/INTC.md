# INTEL CORP (INTC)

## Business model
- Intel designs and manufactures its own chips and also uses third-party foundries (IDM 2.0), and sells foundry capacity to outside customers [N39][N28].
- Intel Foundry is an internal segment. Intel Products buys wafers at fair market prices [N239]. Foundry revenue is about $18B [N285]. Commerce grants of up to $7.86B are paid on milestones [N286].
- Intel plans to sell 51% of Altera to Silver Lake for $4.4B net cash [N310].

## Revenue build-up
- Client (CCG) is PC units times ASP. In Q2 2021 units rose 33% while ASPs fell 15% on mix [N36]. In Q3 2024 CCG fell 1% sequentially on customer inventory work-down [N280].
- Data center (DCAI) is server CPU demand and mix. It rose 10% sequentially in Q3 2024 [N279], then fell 5% in Q1 2025 but beat expectations on host-CPU demand for AI servers [N309]. Management expects double-digit server core growth on flat units in 2025 [N314].
- Foundry revenue was $4.4B in Q1 2024, down 10% [N238]. Near-term external growth is concentrated in advanced packaging [N264].
- Mobileye fell 8% year over year in Q3 2024 as China shipments more than halved [N283]. Altera was expected to exit 2024 at a $2B run rate [N233].

## Core thesis
- **Stale guidance:** the newest material is the 2025-04-24 call, about 19 months old. Treat all figures as dated.
- **Revenue:** Q2 2025 was guided to $11.2–12.4B [N306]. Management planned for a TAM contraction [N313] and must stop data center share loss [N301]. Its sizing assumption is 3–5% trend growth [N274].
- **Margins:** Q2 2025 non-GAAP gross margin was guided to about 36.5% [N307], against 39.2% in Q1 [N315]. Product margin was expected to fall in 2025 on mix and Lunar Lake costs [N292][N299]. Upside needs an EUV wafer mix shift and Foundry break-even by end-2027 [N287][N296]. The long-term target is 60% gross margin [N252].

## What changed over time
- **2021:** IDM 2.0 and the foundry push began [N39][N28]. Gross margin was guided to 51–53% for the next two to three years [N57].
- **2022:** Revenue guidance was cut. DCAI turned to decline, and gross margin guidance fell below the target range [N84][N89][N109][N70].
- **2023:** Intel moved to an internal foundry model and set a $3B cost reduction target [N148][N160].
- **2024:** Foundry became a separate segment. Gross margin guidance stepped down through the year [N239][N249]. The 2025 OpEx target was $17.5B [N250].
- **2025 (April):** Gross capex was cut to about $18B [N304]. Falcon Shores was cut back to an internal test chip [N302]. The Altera stake sale was announced [N310].

## Catalysts
- FY2026 OpEx of about $16B is checkable at year-end results [N303].
- 2026 non-controlling interest is guided to $1.2–1.4B GAAP [N295].
- Foundry break-even operating income is targeted by end-2027 [N296].
- Over $15B of external Foundry revenue and 60%/40% gross/operating margin are targeted by decade end [N277][N252].
- The Altera close, Panther Lake and Clearwater Forest were all dated before 2026-11-21. The notes do not confirm outcomes [N311][N297][N312].

## KPIs to track
- CCG and DCAI revenue, ASPs, and server core growth [N36][N314].
- Non-GAAP gross margin against guidance [N315].
- Foundry operating loss and EUV mix [N254][N287].
- Committed external foundry deals [N261].
- OpEx and gross/net capex [N303][N304][N305].
- Cumulative Core Ultra AI PCs (target above 100M by end-2025) [N289].

## Potentially useful datasets
- **PC shipment and sell-through data (CCG units):** management expects shipments to align with consumption, so sell-through against sell-in shows the inventory correction before it reaches revenue [N188][N146].
- **AI PC share and mix data:** tracks the AI PC driver of client volume and ASPs [N262].
- **Server CPU consumption data:** tracks the DCAI TAM driver [N168].
- **Advanced packaging capacity or volume data:** tracks the near-term foundry growth management describes [N271].
