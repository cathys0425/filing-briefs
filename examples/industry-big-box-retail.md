# Industry view: big-box-retail (WMT, TGT, COST, HD, LOW)

## How the industry makes money
- Merchandise sold at scale on price: Walmart on everyday low prices, Costco on a narrow assortment with low gross margin. [WMT N524][COST N482][COST N492]
- Membership fees recognized ratably at Sam's and Costco. [WMT N592][COST N483]
- Advertising: Target's non-merchandise revenue grew 20.1% in Q2. [TGT N459]
- Home improvement adds Pro distribution through acquisitions (SRS/GMS, FBM/ADG). [HD N503][LOW N479]

## Common revenue drivers and KPIs
- Comps are traffic or transactions × ticket. Target Q2 2026: +3.8%, traffic +3.6 pts. Home Depot Q3 FY26: +2.2%, transactions -0.5%. Lowe's Q2 FY26: +0.2%, transactions -2.1%. [TGT N458][HD N582][HD N583][LOW N506]
- Digital: Walmart eCommerce +23%; Costco digitally-enabled comps +21%; Target digital comps +8.7%. [WMT N596][COST N520][TGT N462]
- Fuel swings reported sales: gasoline was 11% of Costco FY2026 sales, and Sam's FY26 comps lost 1.9 points. [COST N485][WMT N573]
- Gross margin: Target Q1 29.0% vs 28.2%; Home Depot Q1 33.0% vs 33.8%. [TGT N451][HD N565]

## Where the companies differ
- Traffic-led versus ticket-led: Target's comps rest on positive traffic, while Home Depot's are ticket-led as transactions fall. [TGT N444][HD N591]
- Membership: Costco's year-end renewal slipped to 92.3% (U.S. and Canada) in 2025. [COST N404]
- Sourcing: about half of Target's merchandise comes from outside the U.S., while Lowe's is importer of record. [TGT N452][LOW N512]
- Guidance freshness: Costco's latest guidance is the 10-K of 2026-08-30. Walmart's latest sales/EPS guide dates from the FY26 (Feb 2025) call, and Target's EPS guidance is from March 2025. [COST N521][WMT N1022][TGT N910]

## Catalysts shared across the group
- Tariff refunds, all undated. Walmart received $2.9B in the July quarter. Home Depot had received about $730M by August 2. Lowe's recognized about $80M in Q2. Costco expects to pass most of its refunds through as lower prices. [WMT N598][HD N576][LOW N514][COST N522]
- Q4 holiday weighting: Target's Nov–Dec season and Walmart's Q4, its seasonally highest-sales quarter. [TGT N403][WMT N530]
- Housing turnover and rates for home improvement. [HD N526][LOW N442]
- Store growth: Costco plans up to 33 openings in FY2027, and Target about 30 new stores. [COST N521][TGT N435]
