# TARGET CORP (TGT)

## Business model
- Net sales are merchandise sales plus other revenue, mainly advertising and credit card profit-sharing income [N460]. CVS pays perpetual occupancy income [N404].
- Stores fulfill over 97% of merchandise sales [N402]. Nearly all sales are U.S., and Q4 carries a larger share [N419][N403].
- About half of merchandise is sourced outside the U.S., with China the largest source [N452].

## Revenue build-up
- Comparable sales = traffic + average ticket. Q2 2026 comps were +3.8%: traffic +3.6 pts, ticket +0.2 pts [N458].
- Food & beverage is the largest category at 25% of merchandise sales [N450]. Digital was 19.6% of merchandise sales, with digital comps +8.7% [N461][N462].
- Non-merchandise revenue grew 20.1% in Q2, mainly Roundel [N459]. Its advertisers have no long-term commitments [N414].
- Stores reached 2,019, from 1,982 a year earlier [N464].

## Core thesis
- Guidance is stale. The newest numeric EPS guidance ($8.80–$9.80) is from March 2025 [N910]. The newest 2026 guidance, from the 10-K for the year ended 2026-01-31, is only capex of about $5 billion and about 30 new stores [N435][N436]. Both are over six months old, and the Aug 2026 10-Q adds no figures [N465].
- Revenue rises while traffic stays positive (Q1 comps +5.6%, traffic +4.4%) [N444]. It falls if traffic turns negative, as in 2025 (traffic -2.2%) [N429].
- Margin rises with lower markdowns and advertising growth (Q1 gross margin 29.0% vs 28.2%) [N451]. It falls on markdowns, as in 2025, or on new tariffs, whose effect management cannot estimate [N433][N467][N466].

## What changed over time
- 2022: Q2 operating margin fell to 1.2% from 9.8% on markdowns and inventory clearance [N132][N138][N139].
- 2023: comps turned negative (Q2 -5.4%, Q3 -4.9%) while gross margin rebounded to 27.4% in Q3 [N212][N224][N775].
- 2024: paid Target Circle 360 launched and the new Target Circle opened April 7 [N818][N801].
- 2025: comps fell, with Q3 at -2.7%, and card penetration dropped to 16.9% [N390][N391].
- 2026: comps reversed to +5.6% in Q1 and +3.8% in Q2 [N444][N458]. The Supreme Court ruled IEEPA tariffs unauthorized on Feb 20 [N439]. The Ulta shop-in-shop agreement was due to end in August 2026 [N410].

## Catalysts
- Nov–Dec 2026 holiday season, Q4's larger share of sales [N403].
- About 30 new stores and about $5 billion capex over 2026 [N435][N436].
- Undated: IEEPA refund claims (processing began April 20; amount unestimable) and a possible CIT order appeal [N453][N454].
- Undated: further transformation costs, and the IRS audit of 2021–2022 transfer pricing [N441][N426].

## KPIs to track
- Comp traffic vs ticket [N458]
- Gross margin rate [N451]
- Non-merchandise revenue growth [N459]
- Digital comps and share of sales [N461][N462]
- Circle Card penetration (16.9% in Q3 2025) [N391]
- Store count [N464]
- Trailing ROIC (12.4%) [N457]

## Potentially useful datasets
- Store foot traffic: tracks the traffic driver of comps [N468]. Visits can be observed within the quarter, before the 10-Q reports traffic.
- Customs and port import volumes from China: track tariff-exposed sourcing [N412][N428]. They show inbound flow ahead of margin effects.
- App and web activity: tracks digitally originated sales [N399]. Engagement should precede reported digital comps.
- Nov–Dec consumer card spending: tracks the holiday share of sales [N413]. It is available before Q4 results.
