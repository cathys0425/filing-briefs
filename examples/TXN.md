# TEXAS INSTRUMENTS INC (TXN)

## Business model
- Designs and manufactures semiconductors for electronics designers and manufacturers worldwide; it owns fabs and assembly/test sites, and 300mm chips cost about 40% less than 200mm chips. [N528][N530][N531]
- Direct sales, including TI.com, were over 80% of 2025 revenue. [N449]
- Owned capacity makes much of the cost base fixed, so margins move with factory loadings. [N477]
- About 20% of 2025 revenue came from China-headquartered customers, and about 50% shipped into China. [N460]

## Revenue build-up
- Q3 2026 Analog revenue was $4,911M (+32%), led by Signal Chain. Embedded Processing was $858M (+21%). [N532][N533]
- 2025 end markets: industrial about 33%, automotive about 33%, data center about 9%. [N447][N448]
- Management attributes revenue changes to customer demand, seen in shipment volumes. Mix between higher- and lower-priced products also moves revenue and gross profit. [N478][N479]
- Q1 and Q4 are seasonally weaker sequentially than Q2 and Q3. [N535]

## Core thesis
- Revenue follows customer shipment volume. Q3 2026 revenue grew 28% year over year, led by industrial, automotive and data center. It would reverse if demand or ordering falls. [N534][N478]
- Margins rise with factory loadings because costs are fixed. Gross margin was 64.2% in Q3 2026 (57.4% a year earlier). Falling loadings would compress it. [N536][N543]
- Embedded margin should improve as the LFAB ramp lifts loadings. Until then it weighs on Embedded profit. [N491][N493]
- Staleness: the newest revenue/EPS guidance in the notes is the April 2025 call (Q2 2025), more than six months old. The 2026 filings give only capex, tax and qualitative guidance. [N829][N539]

## What changed over time
- 2023: revenue fell 11–14% year over year in Q1–Q3. Gross margin slid to 62.1% in Q3 and inventory days reached 205. [N218][N235][N729][N259][N253]
- 2024: gross margin fell to 58.1% from 62.9%, and Embedded operating margin fell to 13.9% from 29.9%. CHIPS investment-credit cash began arriving in Q2. [N389][N390][N332]
- 2025: revenue rose 13% to $17.68B, and gross margin slipped to 57.0% on capacity-expansion costs. The credit rose from 25% to 35% for assets placed in service after 2025. [N480][N485][N422]
- 2026: gross margin recovered through the year, to 64.2% in Q3. Q3 revenue rose 28%, and trailing free cash flow reached $8.4B. [N518][N536][N534][N538]
- 2026: TI agreed to buy Silicon Labs for $231.00 per share in cash (about $7.5B enterprise value). [N505]

## Catalysts
- Year-end 2026: outcome versus the $2–3B 2026 capex guide, with the six-year elevated cycle ending. [N539][N503]
- 1H 2027: Silicon Labs closing, subject to regulatory and stockholder approvals. It will be partly debt-funded. [N540][N506][N542]
- Undated: LFAB ramp raising loadings. [N524]
- Undated: closure of the two remaining 150mm factories. [N492]
- Undated: remaining CHIPS direct funding (up to $1.6B, $630M received). [N541]

## KPIs to track
- Gross margin (64.2% in Q3 2026) and factory loadings. [N536][N543]
- Analog and Embedded growth, and industrial/automotive/data center mix. [N532][N533][N534]
- Days of inventory (194). [N537]
- Trailing-twelve-month free cash flow ($8.4B) and capex versus guide. [N538][N539]
- Embedded operating margin. [N390]
- China exposure (about 20% by headquarters). [N460]

## Potentially useful datasets
- Semiconductor shipment volumes (customs/logistics counts). They track the volume driver of revenue and are observable before TI reports. [N444][N544]
- Distributor inventory and channel restocking data. They track the timing of inventory adjustments behind revenue swings and show orders turning before sales. [N475]
- TI.com stock availability and lead times. They proxy supply-demand balance and inventory position and move ahead of reported numbers. [N728]
- China-bound semiconductor customs data. They track the roughly 50% of revenue shipped into China, with import flows preceding revenue. [N474]
- Industrial and automotive production indicators. They track the largest end markets, whose output precedes chip demand. [N458][N823]
