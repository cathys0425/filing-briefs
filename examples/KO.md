# COCA COLA CO (KO)

## Business model
- Sells concentrates and syrups to bottlers, who make and sell the finished drinks. Finished-product operations bring higher revenue but lower gross margin. [N788][N789]
- Bottlers must buy their full concentrate requirement for Company Trademark Beverages. [N791]
- Revenue timing depends on ownership. Sales to unconsolidated bottlers count when concentrate is sold. Sales to consolidated bottlers count only after the bottler sells to a third party. [N904][N905]

## Revenue build-up
- Concentrate is about 59% of revenue and 85% of unit case volume. Finished product is about 41% of revenue and 15% of volume. [N833]
- Q3 2026 revenue rose 7%: volume about 7 points, price/mix 1, FX 1, divestitures about -1. [N908]
- Price/mix covers pricing actions plus product, package, channel and territory mix. [N906] Asia Pacific mix and affordability initiatives weighed on it in Q1. [N863]
- Outside the U.S. is 84% of unit case volume. [N795] In Q3, Latin America volume rose 6% and Bottling Investments rose 8%, led by India and Africa. [N910][N911]

## Core thesis
- Volume now leads growth, and it is measured at consumer level. Price/mix slowed from 2 points in Q2 to 1 in Q3. [N907][N882][N908]
- Margin: gross margin rose to 63.0% from 62.6% on pricing, FX and the Nigeria sale, partly offset by commodity costs. [N867] Management expects FX to help full-year revenue and operating income. [N914]
- A stronger U.S. dollar could materially reduce reported results. [N817]

## Catalysts
- Q4 2026 has six fewer days than Q4 2025, which distorts volume and revenue comparisons. [N912]
- An Eleventh Circuit ruling is pending after the June 25, 2026 argument. A loss means no recovery of the $6.0B paid, and about $14B of exposure for 2010–2025. [N930][N928][N929]
- The January 2026 OECD side-by-side Pillar Two guidance could change global minimum tax exposure. [N810]
- African bottling operations are held for sale after a $1,274 million charge. [N858]

## KPIs to track
- Unit case volume growth, and concentrate volume versus unit case volume. Concentrate is guided to trail slightly for 2026. [N907][N913]
- Price/mix points. [N908]
- Gross margin. [N867]
- FX effect on operating income: +3% in Q3, +4% for nine months. [N922]
- Factored receivables: $10.6B in nine months versus $11.8B a year earlier. [N920]
- Buybacks: 10.0M shares at $78.77. [N927]

## Potentially useful datasets
- **Point-of-sale/scanner data for Coca-Cola trademark and category units.** Tracks unit case volume, which management ties to consumer demand. It is observable before the quarterly filing. [N918][N907]
- **Spot FX rates for operating currencies.** Tracks the FX effect on operating income, which management guides as favorable. They are known daily, ahead of reporting. [N931][N914]
- **Regional weather data.** Tracks weather-driven volume swings in the seasonal Q2/Q3 peak, and is observable in real time. [N909][N802]
- **Agricultural commodity prices.** Track input-cost pressure on margin, which commodity costs already offset in part. [N830][N867]
