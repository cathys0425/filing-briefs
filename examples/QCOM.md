# QUALCOMM INC/DE (QCOM)

## Business model
- QCT sells fabless chips (Snapdragon and Dragonwing platforms with processors and modems). QTL licenses patents. QSI makes strategic investments. [N991][N1046][N990]
- QTL royalties are per-unit, a percentage of the licensee's wholesale price, paid quarterly. Fixed-term agreements must be renewed. [N987][N988][N1018]
- QCT orders are cancellable before shipment. Apple and Samsung each made up at least 10% of fiscal 2026 revenue. [N989][N992]

## Revenue build-up
- QCT chips are volume times ASP, with shipments lumpy around device launches. [N897][N898]
- Automotive growth in fiscal 2026 was $1.3B from revenue per unit and $712M from shipments on new launches. [N1047]
- Handsets fell as major OEMs cut chipset shipments to reduce inventory after memory supply constraints and price increases. [N1048]
- Data Center added $97M in fiscal Q2 2026, mainly from Alphawave. [N962]
- QTL is device volume times price. In fiscal 2026, estimated cellular sales fell $194M and revenue per unit rose $238M on mix. [N1005][N1049][N1050]

## Core thesis
- Handset chip revenue falls while memory constraints persist and Apple, Samsung and Xiaomi shift to their own modems or ICs. [N979][N1006][N1007]
- Growth must come from automotive and Data Center (custom silicon ramp from fiscal Q1 2027) to offset that. [N1047][N1051] Mix shifting away from higher-margin licensing weighs on margins. [N1017][N856]
- Gross margin recovers only if price increases pass through. Management expects the full benefit in the second half of fiscal 2027, against rising leading-edge costs. [N1052][N968]
- Staleness: the latest numeric guidance in these notes is the April 2025 call, over six months old. [N1336] The 2026-09-27 10-K gives only qualitative guidance. [N1051][N1053]

## What changed over time
- 2022: Revenue rose 32% to $44.2B. [N396]
- 2023: Revenue fell 19% to $35.8B as chipset shipments to major OEMs dropped $7.9B. [N593][N594]
- 2024: The Huawei export license was revoked (May 7), ending product sales. Automotive hit a record $899M in Q4. [N700][N758][N1302]
- 2025: Transsion and two key Chinese OEMs signed licenses. Huawei's license expired. [N929][N853][N928]
- 2025–26: Alphawave closed December 18, 2025 for $2.3B. Handsets weakened on memory-driven inventory cuts. [N951][N1048]

## Catalysts
- Fiscal Q1 2027: Data Center moves into the QCT reportable segment, with growth expected from custom silicon. [N996][N1051]
- March 2027: Apple's license, extended by a unilateral option, runs to this date. [N1265]
- Near term: capex rises above fiscal 2026 levels. [N1053]
- Second half of fiscal 2027: full gross-margin benefit of price increases expected. [N1052]
- Undated: tariff or trade restrictions, and renewal arbitration provisions. [N1038][N902]

## KPIs to track
- Handset revenue and chipset shipments to major OEMs. [N943][N1048]
- Automotive revenue, shipments and revenue per unit. [N1047]
- QTL estimated cellular sales, revenue per unit and EBT margin (77% in Q1 FY26). [N1049][N1050][N946]
- QCT gross margin. [N963]
- Data Center revenue. [N962]
- Effective tax rate (estimate moved from 15% to a 40% benefit). [N947][N980]

## Potentially useful datasets
- DRAM/NAND price index: tracks handset OEM chip orders. OEM cuts followed memory price increases. [N954][N1048]
- Smartphone shipments by OEM and price tier, including China: tracks handset chipset demand and royalty base. Sell-through versus sell-in shows OEM inventory before QCT reports. [N984][N909][N1043]
- Light-vehicle production and new-model launches with Snapdragon cockpit: tracks automotive shipments. Launches precede shipments. [N985][N938]
